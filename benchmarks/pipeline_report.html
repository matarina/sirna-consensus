EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What the pipeline does. A single transcript FASTA is fanned out to independent open-source design tools. Their heterogeneous outputs are normalised onto transcript coordinates, merged at the level of the exact site (start + length), voted by distinct generator tools, filtered, and ranked into a Top-K panel. An optional frozen OligoFormer scorer and a Bowtie/Ensembl screen add efficacy ranking and computational specificity evidence; neither is a design vote.

What the current evidence shows. On the exposed Huesken development cohort the frozen scorer is genuinely discriminative (AUROC 0.827, AP 0.82, Spearman rho 0.66; decile hit rate 5.2% → 92.3%). Quality fusion reaches 92.8% gene-weighted Top-5 precision versus 90.4% for the model, but the paired gain is +2.4 points with a 95% CI of [-0.8, +5.6] and misses the +10-point criterion. In the final weight-independent holdout (399 evaluable records / 10 labels / 5 studies), policy and model are identical at +0.0 points. The AREG run is reproducible (1,246 hits → 204 sites → 59 two-vote sites → 34 filtered → 10 selected) but its specificity status is input_only.

What is not established. No independent efficacy validation, no evidence that consensus/fusion beats the frozen scorer, and no transcriptome-wide specificity claim. The holdout is weight-independent but upstream-evaluation-exposed; near-duplicate and gene-synonym leakage are not audited. This is a research-grade design aid, not clinical evidence.

5. Leakage and defensibility

Important: the benchmark is honest about exposure but does not eliminate it. Development data is checkpoint-training exposed. The holdout is weight-independent, but every holdout study is upstream-evaluation-exposed; fully_independent_available = false. The audit matches only exact 19-nt antisense cores, not near-duplicates, isoforms or coordinates, and gene synonyms are not merged (Firefly luciferase vs FireflyLuc). The final holdout is small and reporter-heavy, so +0.0 points is not evidence of superiority. The defensible claim is: the scorer is informative on development; the pipeline is reproducible; consensus/fusion superiority is not established.

What is supported

Frozen scorer discrimination: AUROC 0.827, AP 0.82, rho 0.66; decile gradient 5.2% → 92.3% against a 50.2% baseline. Reproducible normalisation, site voting and freezes. Honest evidence-tier labelling.

What is not supported

Independent efficacy validation; consensus/fusion superiority; transcriptome-wide specificity from the AREG run; generalization from reporter/ single-construct holdout labels; any wet-lab knockdown claim.
